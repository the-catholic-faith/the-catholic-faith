THE EUCHARISTIC CELEBRATION

The Holy Mass, step by step

The Mass has two main parts — the Liturgy of the Word and the Liturgy of the Eucharist — framed by the introductory and concluding rites.

01

Introductory Rites

Entrance procession and song, Sign of the Cross, greeting, Penitential Act, Kyrie when appropriate, Gloria on prescribed days, and Collect.

People's response examples

Priest: “The Lord be with you.”
People: “And with your spirit.”
Use the approved wording of your local missal.

02

Liturgy of the Word

First reading, Responsorial Psalm, second reading on Sundays and solemnities, Gospel acclamation, Gospel, homily, Creed when prescribed, and Prayer of the Faithful.

People's response examples

Reader: “The word of the Lord.”
People: “Thanks be to God.”
Before the Gospel: “Glory to you, O Lord.”

03

Liturgy of the Eucharist

Preparation of the gifts, prayer over the offerings, Preface, Holy Holy, Eucharistic Prayer, consecration, Mystery of Faith, doxology, and Great Amen.

People's response examples

All: “Holy, Holy, Holy Lord God of hosts…”
All: “Amen.” at the conclusion of the Eucharistic Prayer.

04

Communion Rite

Lord's Prayer, sign of peace where provided, breaking of the bread, Lamb of God, Communion, silence or hymn, and Prayer after Communion.

Before receiving Communion

The communicant responds “Amen” to “The Body of Christ.” Follow the guidance of your parish and the Church's norms for receiving Communion.

05

Concluding Rites

Brief announcements where appropriate, greeting and blessing, dismissal, and the procession out.

Dismissal response

Deacon or priest: “Go forth, the Mass is ended” (wording varies by approved translation).
People: “Thanks be to God.”

Important: This is an educational overview, not a substitute for the approved Roman Missal or the instructions of your priest. Exact wording and options vary by approved liturgical edition and local norms.

SIGNS OF GRACE

The seven sacraments

The sacraments are central to Catholic life. Explore their meaning and the preparation normally involved.

01

Baptism

New birth in Christ and entry into the Church.

02

Confirmation

Strengthening by the gift of the Holy Spirit.

03

Eucharist

The sacrament at the heart of Catholic worship.

04

Penance & Reconciliation

Forgiveness and reconciliation with God and the Church.

05

Anointing of the Sick

Grace, comfort, and strength in serious illness or old age.

06

Holy Orders

Ordination to the diaconate, priesthood, or episcopate.

07

Matrimony

The covenant of marriage between a baptized man and woman.
